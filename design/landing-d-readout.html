A trainer on the surface. A study underneath.

Can a machine tell a person from a script? Software that aims doesn't overshoot, doesn't correct, doesn't tremble. Those absences are measurable, and a classifier that reads them is the first goal here.

And can it tell one person from another? Harder, more interesting, and the reason your recordings are treated as sensitive rather than as scores.

What that second question needs is repetition across days — three sessions on three days is worth more than thirty in one night.
