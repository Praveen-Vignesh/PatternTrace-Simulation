A trainer on the surface. A study underneath.

Telling a human from a script. Software that aims does not overshoot, does not correct, and does not tremble. Those absences are measurable, and a classifier trained on them is the first goal here.

That work needs both halves: real human aim, and deliberately built synthetic aim to contrast it against.

Telling one human from another. Harder, more interesting, and the reason the recordings are treated as sensitive rather than as scores.

What makes it work is repetition across days, not volume in one sitting. Three sessions on three days is worth more here than thirty in one night.
